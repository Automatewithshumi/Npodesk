YOUR PROPOSAL DOCUMENT STRUCTURE
When you get a big lead, send a formal proposal with these sections:

1. Cover page
   NpoDesk logo + client name + date

2. Executive summary (1 page)
   The problem they have
   How NpoDesk solves it
   The investment required

3. Solution overview (2 pages)
   Screenshots of every module
   List of all features
   POPIA compliance section

4. Implementation plan (1 page)
   Week 1: Setup & data migration
   Week 2: Staff training
   Week 3: Go-live & support
   Week 4: Review & handover

5. Pricing (1 page)
   Setup fee: R___
   Monthly fee: R___/month
   Support tier: R___/month
   Total Year 1: R___

6. Terms & conditions (1 page)
   Payment terms: 50% upfront, 50% on go-live
   Contract period: 12 months minimum
   Notice period: 30 days
   Data ownership: Client owns their data
   Uptime guarantee: 99.5%

7. About NpoDesk (1 page)
   Who you are
   Your mission
   Contact details
